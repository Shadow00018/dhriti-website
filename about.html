About Dhriti Sharma

Some people study the human mind. Dhriti Sharma explores it with empathy, curiosity, and purpose. With a strong academic foundation and hands-on experience, she helps individuals navigate emotions, behaviors, and personal challenges while fostering resilience and self-awareness.

Professional Journey

Dhriti holds a Master’s in Clinical Psychology and has worked extensively with adolescents, students, and individuals facing emotional and behavioral challenges. Her approach combines scientific psychology with human connection, creating a safe space for understanding and growth.

Clinical Experience & Workshops

During her clinical training and internships at leading hospitals, Dhriti gained expertise in psychodynamic therapies, adolescent counseling, behavioral assessment, and stress management. She also conducts workshops on mental wellbeing, academic motivation, and emotional intelligence.

Community Impact

Beyond therapy, Dhriti is passionate about education and social impact. Through NGO initiatives, she has worked with underprivileged children, adapting teaching and counseling techniques to meet diverse learning and emotional needs.

Philosophy & Coaching

Her philosophy is simple yet powerful: mental health care should be compassionate, accessible, and transformative. Alongside her psychological expertise, Dhriti is a certified Life Coach and International Career Coach, guiding individuals toward personal and professional growth.
